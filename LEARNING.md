Few Terminologies to be handy with:
- Primary Sales: Sales from Emami to distributors.
- Secondary Sales: Sales from distributors to retail (consumer demand)
- NSV (Net Sales Value): Sales revenue after deducting discounts and trade schemes.
- ECO (Effective Coverage): Count of distinct retail outlets that placed orders.
- TC / PC: Total Calls (shops visited) vs Productive Calls (shops that placed orders).
- OTIF / OEE: On Time In Full (delivery efficiency) vs Overall Equipment Effectiveness (%age of factory machine utilization)
